Every energy shown as a level on a logarithmic scale, read out in three units at once. The single fact that unlocks the picture: 1 eV ≈ 40 kT at room temperature. Biology works within a handful of kT of the thermal floor, so it is soft and reversible. A covalent bond sits ~140 kT up, effectively permanent at 300 K. Ion beams reach 10³ to 10⁶ kT, energies thermal motion can never supply, which is exactly why implantation and etching need them.
